4 The foil: a generic model of the path

A common shortcut: fit a neural net (frame number and throw → position, 5→16→2) to the recording.

Reach for Handwired when you have a simulation you believe in and measurements it should match: fit its constants, and keep the simulation.

The “recording” is simulated: the same step with hidden constants (GRAVITY 0.5, DRAG 0.012, BOUNCE 0.72) and ±0.4 px of camera jitter, 72 frames. The step is unrolled 72 times into one circuit (2,379 operations); the floor is an exact comparison and select. A bounce is differentiable in its height but not its timing, and gradients through many frames can explode, so learning starts on the first 12 frames and lengthens the window to all 72. Replay is float32 in the net and float64 in JavaScript, so the paths agree to the stated difference, not bit for bit.
