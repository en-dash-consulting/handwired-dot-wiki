      Learn first (step 3) to see the diff.

      Reach for Handwired when you already have a formula people understand, rules that must hold exactly, and data that should tune the numbers, not replace the code.

      Shoppers are simulated with a hidden taste (2·match + 1·pop + 4·fresh + 3·rating, hidden from both models), attention that fades with position (1 / (1 + 0.35 × position)), and less interest in out-of-stock items. Rates are measured live on seeded test lists of 20 results each. The generic model is a 6→8→1 ReLU net, also trained with Adam, in a Web Worker so the page stays responsive. Both train on the clicks of non-pinned, in-stock results, since the rules decide the others.
