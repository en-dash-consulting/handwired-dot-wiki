# Colour themes that learn, and stay accessible

> A theme function learns its constants from a designer's colour picks as a Handwired neural net, while WCAG AA contrast stays exact.

Source: https://handwired.wiki/pages/color-themes.html

<p class="lede">Let a designer point at colours. The theme learns their taste and can't learn to be unreadable.</p>

*(Interactive demo: open https://handwired.wiki/pages/color-themes.html)*
