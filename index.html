Learn Handwired

Turn regular JavaScript into a neural net — no training, exact, yet fully differentiable.

const result = (a > 4) ? 10 : 12
// becomes
const above4 = Math.max(0, a - 4) - Math.max(0, a - 5)
const result = above4 * 10 + (1 - above4) * 12
